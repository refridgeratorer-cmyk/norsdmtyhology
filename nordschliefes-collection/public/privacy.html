What the site stores

If you create an account, the site stores your username, display name, bio, optional profile picture, the votes you cast, and a hashed version of your password (never the password itself). A login cookie keeps you signed in.

What it is used for

Only to run the site: showing your profile, counting votes, and keeping you logged in. Nothing is sold or shared with advertisers.

Google Drive backup

The site owner uses a private Google app ("Collection Backup") that connects only to the owner's own Google Drive. It saves a backup copy of the site's database and uploaded images into one folder. Visitors are never asked to sign in with Google, and the app does not access any visitor's Google data.

Deleting your data

To have your account removed, contact the site owner.
